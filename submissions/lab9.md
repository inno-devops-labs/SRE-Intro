7. **Answer: "What's the RPO of your current setup (single `pg_dump`)? How would you improve it? (Hint: Bonus Task.)"**
	* **Current RPO:** The current Recovery Point Objective is determined by the duration between the manual execution of the pg_dump command and the moment of the database failure. Because the backup process is manual, any data generated by the mixedload script after the dump is created will be permanently lost during a disaster. Therefore, the RPO is variable and depends entirely on the frequency of manual interventions. 
	* **Improvement :** 
		1. **Automation via Kubernetes CronJob:** Scheduling regular, automated dump creations every thirty minutes or every hour establishes a fixed maximum RPO of thirty to sixty minutes. 
		2. **Continuous Archiving with WAL Shipping:** Implementing tools like Wal-g to continuously ship Write-Ahead Logs to external storage enables Point-in-Time Recovery and minimizes the RPO to a matter of seconds or minutes. 
		3. **Persistent Volume Configuration:** Deploying a Persistent Volume Claim for the PostgreSQL pod ensures data persistence across container lifetimes, preventing data erasure during routine pod recreations.

- **Your `k8s/backup-cronjob.yaml` contents.**
	`apiVersion: batch/v1`
	`kind: CronJob`
	`metadata:`
	  `name: postgres-backup`
	`spec:`
	  `schedule: "*/5 * * * *"`
	  `concurrencyPolicy: Forbid`
	  `successfulJobsHistoryLimit: 3`
	  `failedJobsHistoryLimit: 3`
	  `jobTemplate:`
	    `spec:`
	      `template:`
	        `spec:`
	          `restartPolicy: OnFailure`
	          `containers:`
	            `- name: backup`
	              `image: postgres:17-alpine`
	              `env:`
	                `- name: PGHOST`
	                  `value: "postgres"`
	                `- name: PGUSER`
	                  `value: "quickticket"`
	                `- name: PGDATABASE`
	                  `value: "quickticket"`
	                `- name: PGPASSWORD`
	                  `value: "quickticket"`
	              `command:`
	                `- /bin/sh`
	                `- -c`
	                `- |`
	                  `echo "Starting pg_dump..."`
	                  `pg_dump -Fc > /backups/quickticket_$(date +%s).dump`
	                  `echo "Rotating old backups (keeping exactly 5)..."`
	                  `cd /backups`
	                  `ls -1t quickticket_*.dump | tail -n +6 | xargs -r rm -v`
	                  `echo "Done."`
	              `volumeMounts:`
	                `- name: backups`
	                  `mountPath: /backups`
	          `volumes:`
	            `- name: backups`
	              `persistentVolumeClaim:`
	                `claimName: postgres-backups`
